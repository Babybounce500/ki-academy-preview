Master the art of designing, refining, and maximizing AI prompts

Chapter 1: Introduction to Prompt Engineering

Prompt Engineering is both an art and a science. It involves formulating questions, directives, or statements in such a way that an AI model can generate coherent, relevant, and context-aware responses. In this book, we'll explore strategies to create effective prompts, discuss use cases for various industries and tasks, and delve into advanced techniques for refining and scaling your prompts.

As AI language models grow more capable, the importance of precise and thoughtful prompt design becomes paramount. Whether you're looking to streamline business processes, craft compelling creative content, or build powerful educational tools, this guide aims to provide you with comprehensive insights, strategies, and examples.

AI Assistants

Harness AI to perform a range of tasks—from customer service to travel planning—while delivering context-aware and human-like assistance.

Use Case Prompt
Customer Service Basic Prompt:
"Act as a customer service agent for an online store. Address a complaint about a delayed order and offer resolution options."

Expanded Prompt:
"You are an empathetic, resourceful customer service representative. The customer has not received their order after two weeks. Apologize politely, explain possible causes (warehouse delays, shipping issues, etc.), and outline two possible resolutions (refund or reshipment). Your tone should be calm, understanding, and solution-focused."
Personal Assistant Basic Prompt:
"Plan a daily schedule for a user who needs 8 hours of sleep and 1 hour of exercise."

Expanded Prompt:
"You are a personal AI assistant. The user has a 9-hour workday (including lunch break), 1 hour of exercise, a 30-minute commute, and wants to meditate for 15 minutes before bed. Create a detailed timetable including wake-up time, meals, and 2 short work breaks. The schedule should minimize stress and ensure work-life balance."
Travel Planner Basic Prompt:
"Create a 7-day itinerary for a couple visiting Rome for the first time."

Expanded Prompt:
"You are a travel-savvy AI, planning a romantic 7-day trip to Rome for a couple on a moderate budget. Include famous historical sites, hidden local gems, recommended eateries, and at least one day trip outside Rome. Provide tips on best times to visit major attractions and how to use public transport effectively."
Meeting Coordinator Basic Prompt:
"Organize a remote team meeting considering different time zones."

Expanded Prompt:
"You are an AI team coordinator. One member is in San Francisco (PST), another in London (GMT), and another in Sydney (AEST). Propose three possible meeting times that overlap conveniently. Include a brief agenda for project updates and a brainstorming session, and note how to record action items for follow-up."
Fitness/Diet Assistant Basic Prompt:
"Suggest a week's worth of balanced meals for someone looking to lose weight."

Expanded Prompt:
"You are a knowledgeable AI nutritionist. The user wants a 7-day meal plan aiming for a daily intake of ~1800 calories. Include breakfast, lunch, dinner, and two snack options per day. Prioritize fresh, minimally processed foods, and list the approximate macronutrient breakdown for each meal. Also offer vegetarian substitutions."
Study Assistant Basic Prompt:
"Help a student create a study plan for finals week covering math, history, and biology."

Expanded Prompt:
"You are an advanced AI tutor. The student has final exams in math, history, and biology in 10 days. They can study 3 hours on weekdays and 5 hours on weekends. Create a study schedule that allocates time by subject, includes quick review quizzes, and suggests specific break intervals. Provide short, motivational tips to reduce stress."

  • Use Context Wisely: Provide relevant background information to help the AI understand nuances or specialized vocabulary.
  • Layered Instructions: For complex tasks, break down instructions into smaller, sequential steps.
  • Example Inputs and Outputs: Show examples to guide the AI’s response more effectively.
  • Guardrails: When necessary, specify what the AI should or shouldn’t include (e.g., limiting sensitive data or personal information).
